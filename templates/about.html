About me

Howdy! My name is Seth Mueller and I am currently a high school Senior in Austin, Texas. I am very passionate about learning by making things helping others learn cool things, which is part of the reason why I made this website. Here are some things about me if you're bored enough to read this page:

  • Interests: Ham radio, electronics, synthesizer design, computer architecture, digital communications, and computer science
  • Favorite editor: Vim or VS Code with the Vim extension (I love macros <3)
  • Favorite programming languages: Python for most quick things, C for embedded, and Rust and Uiua because they're fun (although I don't use these often)
  • Favorite drink: Americano coffee
  • Favorite video game: Counter Strike 2
  • President of the K5LBJ amateur radio club. Stop by sometime if you go to my school!

Cool links:

  • meow mix - meowballz - Best video on the webz!?
  • Falstad circuit simulator - Great real-time simulator for messing around with simple analog circuit design
  • All About Circuits textbook - Good for getting started in electronics
  • Shadertoy - Make art out of code with the c-like GLSL language!
  • Inigo Quilez distance functions - Super useful tools for shader graphics programming (above)! Lots of cool tutorials on his site as well
  • UIUA programming language - Freaky developmental array-based language with tons of special characters (like APL)
  • Soundcard OFDM - Awesome intro to modern digital communications (think cell, WiFi, Bluetooth) techniques using Python!

Book Recs:

  • The Art of Electronics by Horowitz and Hill - Very well written book serving as a good intermediate intro to practical circuit design.
  • Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach by Hennessy and Patterson - Awesome overview of many modern computer engineering topics and techniques, rarely feels dull while reading
  • Visual Complex Analysis by Tristan Needham - Still going through this one, but it has been fun so far with really good explanations and intuitive derivations of the concepts
